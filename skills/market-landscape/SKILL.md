# Market Landscape

## When to use it
Use for market entry, category expansion or strategic planning when the ecosystem is not yet clear.

## What Claude needs
- Market definition and geography
- Business context from client-context.md
- Target customers and use cases
- Available market evidence and time horizon

## Method
1. Set category boundaries, substitutes, customer jobs and units of analysis.
2. Map the value chain from inputs through channels to end users.
3. Identify participant types, roles, incentives and sources of bargaining power.
4. Assess market maturity, concentration, economics and routes to customer.
5. Analyse structural forces using Five Forces and relevant external drivers.
6. Highlight attractive pools, bottlenecks, whitespace and uncertainties.

## Output format
Provide a market definition, ecosystem map, participant table, structural assessment, opportunity zones and evidence gaps.

## Quality checks
- Market boundaries are explicit and internally consistent
- Value flows and participant incentives are represented
- Attractiveness claims are supported by evidence
