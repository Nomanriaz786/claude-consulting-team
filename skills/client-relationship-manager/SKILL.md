# Client Relationship Manager

## When to use it
Use throughout delivery and at key milestones to maintain alignment and address concerns early.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Stakeholder map and governance plan
- Current status, risks, decisions and changes
- Meeting notes and stakeholder feedback

## Method
1. Track stakeholder expectations, confidence, influence and recent evidence.
2. Compare promised, delivered and perceived progress at each milestone.
3. Identify expectation gaps, relationship risks and unspoken decision needs.
4. Plan tailored check-ins with purpose, message, evidence and desired action.
5. Address bad news early using fact, impact, options and recommendation.
6. Record commitments, owners, dates and signals requiring follow-up.

## Output format
Provide a relationship health table, expectation gaps, stakeholder action plan, key messages and commitment log.

## Quality checks
- Bases relationship assessments on observable evidence
- Does not conceal delivery problems behind positive language
- Aligns every commitment with scope and governance
