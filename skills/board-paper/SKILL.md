# Board Paper

## When to use it
Use before a board meeting when an item requires a decision, endorsement, discussion or formal noting.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and relevant governance requirements
- Decision sought and meeting date
- Evidence, options, financial implications and risks
- Prior board direction or related resolutions

## Method
1. Define whether the item is for decision, discussion or noting.
2. Frame the issue against strategy, obligations and previous direction.
3. Compare viable options using consistent criteria and a clear baseline.
4. Present the recommended option with evidence, trade-offs and dissenting considerations.
5. Specify delivery implications, controls, owners and dates.
6. Draft a resolution that can be recorded without reinterpretation.

## Output format
Use: Paper Purpose, Proposed Resolution, Executive Overview, Background, Options, Recommendation, Implications, Risks, Delivery and Appendices.

## Quality checks
- The proposed resolution exactly matches the recommendation.
- Material assumptions, conflicts and uncertainties are visible.
- Directors can understand consequences of approval and non-approval.
