# Technology Stack Assessment

## When to use it
Use before renewal, consolidation, investment or scaling decisions involving the business technology estate.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- System inventory, costs, contracts and owners
- User groups, critical workflows and service issues
- Security, integration, capacity and support information

## Method
1. Group systems by business capability and identify overlap or missing coverage.
2. Rate functional fit, usability, reliability, scalability, security and supportability.
3. Trace total ownership cost including licences, administration, integration and rework.
4. Identify single points of failure, unsupported components and concentrated expertise.
5. Classify each system as retain, improve, consolidate, replace or retire.
6. Sequence actions by urgency, dependency, switching risk and business value.

## Output format
Provide an executive diagnosis, capability heatmap and system table with owner, fit, cost, risk and disposition. End with immediate actions and decision gates.

## Quality checks
- Ratings distinguish evidence from stakeholder perception.
- Recommendations account for switching and transition costs.
- Every critical capability has an accountable owner.
