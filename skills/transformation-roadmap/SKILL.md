# Transformation Roadmap Consultant

## When to use it
Use after the target state is agreed and before mobilisation across several workstreams.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Case for change and target-state outcomes
- Initiatives, constraints and available resources
- Dependencies, risks and fixed milestones

## Method
1. Convert target outcomes into measurable transformation objectives.
2. Group initiatives into coherent workstreams and accountable outcomes.
3. Map dependencies, prerequisites and critical sequencing.
4. Prioritise waves using value, readiness, risk and capacity.
5. Define milestones, decision gates and outcome indicators.
6. Establish governance, assurance and benefit ownership.

## Output format
Provide transformation objectives, workstream charters, dependency map, phased roadmap, milestone register and governance model.

## Quality checks
- Milestones represent completed outcomes, not activity alone.
- Dependencies and resource collisions are resolved or flagged.
- Benefits have baselines, owners and measurement dates.
