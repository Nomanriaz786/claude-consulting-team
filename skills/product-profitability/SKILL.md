# Product Profitability

## When to use it
Use when product revenue is growing without clear profit, or portfolio choices need financial evidence.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Revenue, discounts and returns by product
- Direct material, labour, fulfilment and support costs
- Product volumes, usage and service requirements
- Shared-cost pools and allocation drivers

## Method
1. Define the product unit and reconcile product revenue to company totals.
2. Assign direct costs and calculate contribution before shared overhead.
3. Allocate only decision-relevant shared costs with stated causal drivers.
4. Segment products by contribution amount, margin and resource intensity.
5. Test price, volume, mix and cost sensitivity for weak and strong products.
6. Classify actions as grow, fix, harvest or retire with dependencies.

## Output format
Provide a product table with revenue, direct cost, contribution, allocated cost and profit. Add a portfolio matrix and action priorities.

## Quality checks
- Product results reconcile to total revenue and cost.
- Shared-cost allocations are visible and reversible.
- Retirement analysis includes stranded and avoidable costs.
