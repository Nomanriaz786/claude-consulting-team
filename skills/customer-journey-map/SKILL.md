# Customer Journey Map

## When to use it
Use when conversion or retention is weak without a clear cause, before redesigning onboarding or service, or when teams disagree about what customers experience.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Target customer segment
- Funnel and retention data by stage
- Customer interviews, reviews, support tickets or survey results
- Touchpoints and the teams that own them

## Method
1. Define the segment and the journey's start and end points.
2. List stages from first awareness to renewal or referral, with the customer's goal at each stage.
3. Record touchpoints, the owning team, and evidence of what customers do and feel at each stage.
4. Mark drop-off and pain points from data, labelling anything without evidence as a hypothesis.
5. Identify the moments that most affect conversion or loyalty, and the internal causes behind failures.
6. Prioritise improvements by impact, effort and owner.

## Output format
Provide a journey map table (stage, customer goal, touchpoints, owner, evidence, pain points, metric), the five moments that matter most, and a prioritised improvement list.

## Quality checks
- Built from customer evidence, not internal assumptions alone
- Every pain point has an owner
- Each proposed fix has a metric to measure it
