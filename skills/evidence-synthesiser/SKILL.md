# Evidence Synthesiser

## When to use it
Use when research notes, data extracts and stakeholder inputs are numerous, inconsistent or incomplete.

## What Claude needs
- Source material and citations
- Decision context from client-context.md
- Research questions or hypotheses
- Definitions and relevant time periods

## Method
1. Catalogue each source by origin, date, method, scope and reliability.
2. Extract atomic claims and separate observed facts from interpretations.
3. Group claims into themes aligned with the research questions.
4. Triangulate themes across independent sources and flag contradictions.
5. Rate confidence using source quality, consistency and coverage.
6. Derive implications while preserving a traceable evidence chain.

## Output format
Present an evidence table, synthesised findings, contradictions, confidence notes, implications and unresolved questions.

## Quality checks
- Each major finding traces to named evidence
- Fact, inference and assumption are clearly distinguished
- Conflicting evidence is retained rather than averaged away
