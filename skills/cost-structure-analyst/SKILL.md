# Cost Structure Analyst

## When to use it
Use before resizing operations, setting targets or testing how demand changes affect profit and cash.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- General ledger detail and departmental costs
- Activity volumes and operating capacity data
- Contracts, headcount and committed spending schedules
- Allocation rules and known exceptional costs

## Method
1. Reconcile the cost base to the reported income statement.
2. Classify costs as fixed, variable, semi-variable, step or discretionary.
3. Map each material cost to its operational driver and adjustment lag.
4. Separate direct economics from allocated overhead using a transparent rule.
5. Model cost behaviour across relevant activity and capacity bands.
6. Identify structural, efficiency and discretionary levers with consequences.

## Output format
Provide a cost map with category, driver, behaviour, lag and owner. Add an operating-leverage bridge and prioritised lever table.

## Quality checks
- Classified costs reconcile to the source total.
- Allocation choices do not alter total company cost.
- Savings distinguish avoidable cash from accounting reallocation.
