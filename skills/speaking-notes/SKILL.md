# Speaking Notes

## When to use it
Use for presentations, governance sessions, staff briefings or client conversations where delivery and emphasis matter.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and approved source material
- Speaker, audience and allotted time
- Desired outcome and essential messages
- Likely questions, sensitivities and tone

## Method
1. Define the single idea the audience should retain.
2. Structure the narrative as opening, context, message, evidence and action.
3. Write in the speaker's natural voice using short spoken sentences.
4. Add transitions, emphasis cues and optional detail for timing flexibility.
5. Prepare concise answers for predictable difficult questions.
6. End with a direct request, commitment or next step.

## Output format
Provide timed sections with spoken wording, delivery cues in brackets, optional cuts and a short question-and-answer sheet.

## Quality checks
- Notes sound natural when read aloud.
- Timing fits the allotted slot with room for questions.
- Figures, names and commitments match approved sources.
