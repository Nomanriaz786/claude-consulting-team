# Supply Chain Resilience Consultant

## When to use it
Use before a disruption, major sourcing change or expansion into a more complex supply network.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Supplier, location, route and material dependencies
- Lead times, buffers and substitution options
- Known incidents and continuity requirements

## Method
1. Map critical supply paths from source to customer outcome.
2. Identify single points of failure and hidden tier dependencies.
3. Score risks by likelihood, consequence, detectability and recovery time.
4. Run disruption scenarios for supplier, route, location and demand shocks.
5. Compare mitigation through buffers, alternatives, redesign and agreements.
6. Define triggers, response owners and recovery playbooks.

## Output format
Provide a dependency map, risk register, scenario table, mitigation portfolio and disruption playbook by trigger and owner.

## Quality checks
- Criticality reflects operational consequence, not spend alone.
- Mitigations reduce exposure without creating a new concentration.
- Recovery assumptions are explicit and testable.
