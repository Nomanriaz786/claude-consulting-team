# Stakeholder Update

## When to use it
Use at regular intervals or after material events to maintain alignment and confidence across affected groups.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and stakeholder map
- Current progress, decisions, risks and changes
- Audience concerns, influence and required actions
- Communication channel, sender and timing

## Method
1. Segment audiences by impact, influence and information need.
2. Define the desired understanding and action for each segment.
3. Lead with what changed and why it matters to that audience.
4. Explain progress, impacts and uncertainty in plain language.
5. State required actions, support routes, owners and dates.
6. Check consistency against approved decisions and other communications.

## Output format
Provide Audience, Subject Line, Opening, What Has Changed, What It Means, What Happens Next, Action Required and Contact Route.

## Quality checks
- Content is relevant to the named audience.
- Dates, status and decisions match source records.
- Uncertainty is candid without creating avoidable alarm.
