# Investor Update

## When to use it
Use monthly or quarterly for existing investors, after a significant event, or in the months before a new funding round.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Key metrics for the period, with prior period and target
- Cash balance, monthly burn and runway
- Wins, setbacks and decisions taken
- Specific help wanted from investors

## Method
1. Open with a three-line summary: overall status, the headline number and the main ask.
2. Report the same core metrics every time, with change from last period and against plan.
3. State cash, monthly burn and runway in months.
4. Cover two or three wins and the most important problem, with what is being done about it.
5. Make specific, actionable asks such as introductions, hires or expertise.
6. Keep the update short enough to read in under three minutes.

## Output format
Provide an investor update with summary, metrics table, cash and runway, highlights, challenges, asks and next-period priorities.

## Quality checks
- Bad news is reported as plainly as good news
- Metrics use the same definitions as previous updates
- Every ask is specific enough to act on
