# Executive Summary

## When to use it
Use for plans, assessments or project material that senior readers must understand without reading the full source.

## What Claude needs
- Source material and client-context.md for background
- Intended readers and their prior knowledge
- Decision, action or purpose of the summary
- Length and tone constraints

## Method
1. Identify the central question, answer and intended executive action.
2. Extract only material findings, evidence, implications and uncertainties.
3. Group points into a MECE narrative of context, insight and response.
4. Lead with the conclusion, then support it with ranked evidence.
5. State decisions, owners and timing explicitly, removing detail that belongs in appendices.

## Output format
Use headings: Purpose, Headline, Key Findings, Implications, Recommendation and Decisions Required. Keep each section concise and use bullets where scanning matters.

## Quality checks
- Every statement is traceable to supplied material.
- The requested decision and next action are unmistakable.
- Detail, repetition and unexplained jargon are removed.
