# Vendor Selection Consultant

## When to use it
Use before requesting bids, comparing finalists or replacing a critical supplier.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Business need, scope and required outcomes
- Mandatory requirements and operational constraints
- Candidate information, references and transition considerations

## Method
1. Separate mandatory gates from weighted evaluation criteria.
2. Translate needs into testable requirements and evidence requests.
3. Weight criteria across capability, service, resilience, fit and total cost.
4. Score candidates consistently and document evidence and uncertainty.
5. Conduct reference, dependency and implementation-risk checks.
6. Test the recommendation with sensitivity and pre-mortem analysis.

## Output format
Provide requirements, gating results, weighted scorecard, risk comparison, recommendation and negotiation points.

## Quality checks
- Criteria were set before candidate scoring.
- Scores cite comparable evidence.
- The recommendation remains sound under plausible weighting changes.
