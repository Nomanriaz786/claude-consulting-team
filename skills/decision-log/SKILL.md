# Decision Log

## When to use it
Use across governance cycles to prevent repeated debate, lost rationale and unclear authority.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and governance structure
- Meeting records, papers and prior decision records
- Decision rights and approval thresholds
- Related actions, dependencies and due dates

## Method
1. Extract decisions separately from discussion, advice and provisional direction.
2. Assign a unique reference and concise decision statement.
3. Record the decision maker, date, authority and source record.
4. Capture rationale, alternatives rejected and material assumptions.
5. Link resulting actions, dependencies and review triggers.
6. Flag conflicts, superseded decisions and gaps for confirmation.

## Output format
Use a table with Decision ID, Date, Decision, Rationale, Authority, Owner, Actions, Dependencies, Review Trigger, Status and Source.

## Quality checks
- Each entry records an actual choice in unambiguous language.
- Decision authority and source can be verified.
- Superseded or conditional entries remain traceable.
