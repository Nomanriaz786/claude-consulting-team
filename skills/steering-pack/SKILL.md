# Steering Committee Pack

## When to use it
Use for recurring governance meetings overseeing a programme, transformation or critical initiative.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and governance cadence
- Plan, milestones, budget position and benefits status
- Current risks, issues, dependencies and decisions
- Previous actions and steering directions

## Method
1. Reconcile current data to the approved baseline and prior pack.
2. Apply agreed red, amber and green thresholds consistently.
3. Lead with material variance, root cause, impact and recovery action.
4. Separate items for decision from items for awareness.
5. Update the action, decision, risk and dependency records.
6. Sequence the agenda around the highest-value steering interventions.

## Output format
Create sections for Executive Snapshot, Decisions Required, Milestones, Delivery Health, Benefits, Risks and Issues, Dependencies and Action Record.

## Quality checks
- Status ratings agree with the supporting evidence.
- Every decision request names an owner and latest decision date.
- Prior actions are closed, carried forward or escalated explicitly.
