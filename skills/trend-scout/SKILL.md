# Trend Scout

## When to use it
Use during strategic planning or market assessment when conditions are changing and timing matters.

## What Claude needs
- Strategic question and planning horizon
- Market context from client-context.md
- Relevant geographic and customer scope
- Sources, observations and suspected signals

## Method
1. Scan political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal drivers.
2. Separate durable trends, emerging signals, cyclical effects and speculation.
3. Trace each trend to customer behaviour, industry economics and value-chain effects.
4. Rate direction, maturity, uncertainty, impact and likely timing.
5. Identify interactions that could amplify, constrain or reverse effects.
6. Define leading indicators and strategic watch items.

## Output format
Provide a trend radar, impact table, causal implications, leading indicators and priority watchlist.

## Quality checks
- Trends are supported by dated, relevant evidence
- Impact pathways are explicit rather than asserted
- Uncertainty and alternative interpretations are visible
