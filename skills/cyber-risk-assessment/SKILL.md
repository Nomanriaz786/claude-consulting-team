# Cyber Risk Assessment

## When to use it
Use during annual planning, after material change, before external assurance or following a significant incident.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Critical assets, systems, data and service dependencies
- Current safeguards, incidents and testing evidence
- Threat concerns, risk criteria and accountable owners

## Method
1. Identify crown-jewel assets and rank their confidentiality, integrity and availability needs.
2. Build plausible threat scenarios linking actor, attack path, asset and business consequence.
3. Evaluate inherent likelihood and impact using defined qualitative scales.
4. Map current preventive, detective and recovery controls to each scenario.
5. Assess residual exposure and test assumptions through a pre-mortem workshop.
6. Prioritise avoid, reduce, transfer or accept treatments with owners and dates.

## Output format
Provide an asset summary, scenario-based risk register and treatment plan with control, gap, residual rating, owner and due date.

## Quality checks
- Risks are written as cause, event and consequence.
- Ratings follow stated criteria and cite evidence.
- Treatments include validation measures and residual ownership.
