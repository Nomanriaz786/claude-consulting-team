# Sales Capacity Model

## When to use it
Use during target setting, territory planning or before changing commercial headcount.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Revenue target by period and segment
- Seller headcount, start dates, roles and territories
- Historical productivity, ramp and attrition data
- Funnel conversion, deal size and cycle time

## Method
1. Convert revenue targets into bookings and timing requirements.
2. Segment sellers into tenured, ramping, new and unavailable capacity.
3. Estimate attainable productivity from comparable historical cohorts.
4. Translate bookings into opportunity and pipeline requirements by stage.
5. Model hiring lead time, ramp, attrition and capacity constraints.
6. Identify gaps and compare hiring, productivity, coverage and target choices.

## Output format
Provide a period capacity model, target-to-pipeline bridge and gap scenarios. Show headcount, productive capacity, expected bookings and shortfall.

## Quality checks
- Booking timing aligns with revenue recognition assumptions.
- Ramping staff are not assigned full productivity early.
- Capacity and funnel calculations reconcile end to end.
