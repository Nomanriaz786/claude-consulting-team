# Opportunity Prioritiser

## When to use it
Use after opportunity discovery and before deeper business cases or resource commitments.

## What Claude needs
- Candidate opportunities
- Strategic context from client-context.md
- Market, customer and competitor evidence
- Constraints, capabilities and decision criteria

## Method
1. Define each opportunity at a consistent level of scope.
2. Agree criteria covering attractiveness, fit, feasibility, risk and timing.
3. Set criterion weights and scoring anchors before assessing options.
4. Score each opportunity with evidence notes and confidence ratings.
5. Run sensitivity tests on weights and uncertain scores.
6. Place options into pursue, explore, monitor or stop categories.

## Output format
Provide criteria definitions, a weighted scorecard, confidence notes, sensitivity findings and a prioritised opportunity portfolio.

## Quality checks
- Scores use common anchors across all options
- High scores are not used to conceal weak evidence
- Ranking remains sensible under plausible weighting changes
