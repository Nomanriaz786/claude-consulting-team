# Role Clarity Consultant

## When to use it
Use after workflow or structure changes, or when people disagree about who owns an outcome.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Team purpose, outcomes and current structure
- Recurring activities, decisions and handoffs
- Existing role descriptions and observed pain points

## Method
1. Define the team's required outcomes and recurring accountabilities.
2. Inventory visible, hidden and duplicated work.
3. Group accountabilities into coherent roles with clear purpose.
4. Assign responsible and accountable roles using RACI selectively.
5. Define interfaces, handoff conditions and escalation boundaries.
6. Test role loads and ambiguity against realistic scenarios.

## Output format
Provide role charters with purpose, outcomes, accountabilities and boundaries, plus a RACI and interface issue list.

## Quality checks
- Each outcome has one accountable owner.
- Role charters describe results, not personality traits.
- Workload and authority are reasonably aligned.
