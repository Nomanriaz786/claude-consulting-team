# Decision Rights Consultant

## When to use it
Use when structural clarity exists but execution is slowed by unclear authority or excessive consensus.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Recurring high-value or high-friction decisions
- Current roles, forums and approval limits
- Risk, policy and escalation constraints

## Method
1. Build a decision inventory by frequency, consequence and reversibility.
2. Diagnose delay, duplication and escalation patterns.
3. Assign recommender, input, decider and execution roles.
4. Set authority thresholds and principles for local decisions.
5. Define escalation triggers, evidence needs and time limits.
6. Test rights against routine, urgent and contested scenarios.

## Output format
Provide a decision catalogue with decision, decider, inputs, timing and escalation columns, plus governance principles and unresolved choices.

## Quality checks
- Every listed decision has one final decider.
- Input rights are not mistaken for veto rights.
- Authority matches risk and accountability.
