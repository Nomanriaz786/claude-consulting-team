# Statement of Work

## When to use it
Use after proposal alignment and before delivery begins, alongside appropriate legal terms.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Approved proposal and negotiated changes
- Deliverables, acceptance criteria and schedule
- Fees, dependencies and governing agreement details

## Method
1. Reconcile all approved terms against the latest scope and fee version.
2. Define services, deliverables, milestones and acceptance procedure precisely.
3. Allocate supplier and client responsibilities using a RACI.
4. State assumptions, dependencies, exclusions and access requirements.
5. Specify fees, invoicing, expenses, change control and termination effects.
6. Flag legal or ambiguous matters for qualified human review.

## Output format
Provide numbered clauses for purpose, scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibilities, acceptance, fees, changes, assumptions and signatures.

## Quality checks
- Uses consistent names, dates and defined terms
- Contains no commitments absent from the approved offer
- Flags legal uncertainty instead of inventing language
