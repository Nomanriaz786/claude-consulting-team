# Discovery Synthesis

## When to use it
Use after calls, interviews or workshops before recommending an engagement path.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Notes, transcripts or workshop outputs
- Original hypotheses and meeting objectives
- Any supplied documents or measures

## Method
1. Normalise notes into facts, statements, interpretations, decisions and actions.
2. Cluster evidence by trigger, problem, impact, stakeholders, constraints and desired outcomes.
3. Compare the evidence with original hypotheses and mark supported, weakened or unresolved.
4. Identify contradictions, missing voices and claims requiring verification.
5. Draft a concise situation-complication-question narrative.
6. Recommend the next decision and the minimum evidence needed to make it.

## Output format
Provide an evidence digest, hypothesis table, contradictions, open questions, agreed actions and situation-complication-question summary.

## Quality checks
- Preserves attribution for material claims
- Separates interpretation from client-stated facts
- Does not hide contradictory evidence
