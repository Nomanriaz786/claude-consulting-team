# Positioning Architect

## When to use it
Use before messaging or market entry when the category, audience or differentiation remains unclear.

## What Claude needs
- Customer segments and value proposition
- Business context from client-context.md
- Competitor and alternative evidence
- Category conventions and credible proof

## Method
1. Identify the best-fit customers and their urgent purchase context.
2. List the alternatives customers would use if the offering did not exist.
3. Isolate differentiated capabilities and translate them into customer value.
4. Choose a market category that makes the value easy to understand.
5. Write a positioning statement with audience, frame, value, difference and proof.
6. Stress-test the position for relevance, credibility, distinctiveness and durability.

## Output format
Provide positioning inputs, alternatives table, category choice, positioning statement, proof hierarchy and stress-test findings.

## Quality checks
- Positioning is relative to real customer alternatives
- Category choice clarifies rather than exaggerates the offer
- Every differentiating claim has credible support
