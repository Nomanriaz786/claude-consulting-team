# Closeout Pack

## When to use it
Use at formal closure after outputs are delivered and operational ownership has transferred.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and approved scope
- Baseline objectives, milestones and acceptance criteria
- Final outcomes, expenditure position and benefit evidence
- Open actions, residual risks and stakeholder input

## Method
1. Compare delivered outcomes with scope, baseline and acceptance criteria.
2. Reconcile milestones, resources, changes and outstanding obligations.
3. Assess expected benefits and schedule remaining measurement points.
4. Run a structured retrospective across strengths, gaps and root causes.
5. Transfer residual risks and actions to named operational owners.
6. Record acceptance, archive requirements and closure approvals.

## Output format
Use: Closure Summary, Objectives and Outcomes, Delivery Performance, Benefits Position, Lessons, Residual Risks, Open Actions, Archive Index and Sign-off.

## Quality checks
- Outcome claims are supported by acceptance evidence.
- Every residual item has an owner and due date.
- Closure status matches contractual and governance requirements.
