# Milestone Plan

## When to use it
Use to turn broad phases into governable commitments without building a full task schedule.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and implementation roadmap
- Required outcomes and target dates
- Workstream owners and delivery constraints
- Dependencies, approvals and quality standards

## Method
1. Break each roadmap phase into observable outcome milestones.
2. Define acceptance criteria and evidence required for completion.
3. Map predecessor, successor and external dependencies.
4. Assign one accountable owner and supporting contributors per milestone.
5. Identify the critical path and schedule decision gates.
6. Add recovery triggers for milestones at risk of delay.

## Output format
Use a table with Milestone, Outcome, Acceptance Evidence, Owner, Contributors, Target Date, Dependencies, Gate and Recovery Trigger.

## Quality checks
- Milestones describe completed outcomes, not ongoing activity.
- Acceptance criteria can be tested objectively.
- Dates, ownership and dependencies are internally consistent.
