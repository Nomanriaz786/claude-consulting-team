# Stakeholder Mapping

## When to use it
Use during qualification, scoping or mobilisation when multiple client voices affect approval and delivery.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Named stakeholders, roles and known relationships
- Decision process and engagement goals
- Meeting notes showing interests or concerns

## Method
1. List stakeholders and label economic buyer, sponsor, champion, user, approver and blocker roles.
2. Assess each person's influence, interest, current stance and evidence confidence.
3. Plot stakeholders on an influence-interest matrix.
4. Identify alignment gaps, missing voices and single-point dependencies.
5. Set an engagement objective, key message and owner for each priority stakeholder.
6. Sequence conversations around actual decision gates and delivery milestones.

## Output format
Provide a stakeholder register, influence-interest matrix, engagement plan and unresolved relationship questions.

## Quality checks
- Separates formal authority from informal influence
- Avoids assigning motives without evidence
- Gives every priority stakeholder an owner and next action
