# Research Brief

## When to use it
Use before commissioning desk research, fieldwork, surveys or expert conversations.

## What Claude needs
- The decision the research must inform
- Relevant context from client-context.md
- Hypotheses or unknowns to test
- Audience, deadline and available resources
- Existing sources and evidence gaps

## Method
1. Translate the decision into primary and secondary research questions.
2. Define scope boundaries by market, customer, geography, channel and time.
3. Select suitable primary and secondary methods for each question.
4. Specify source standards, sample logic and triangulation requirements.
5. Create a workplan with dependencies, checkpoints and stop conditions.
6. Record likely bias, access constraints and ethical considerations.

## Output format
Provide sections for decision context, objectives, questions, scope, methods, source standards, workplan, risks and deliverables.

## Quality checks
- Every research question links to the decision
- Methods are proportionate to the confidence required
- Scope exclusions and evidence limitations are explicit
