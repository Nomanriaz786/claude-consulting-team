# Risk Escalation

## When to use it
Use when normal controls cannot contain exposure within agreed authority, time or tolerance.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and risk tolerances
- Risk or issue evidence and current assessment
- Existing controls, actions and accountable owners
- Escalation route, decision deadline and available options

## Method
1. State the risk event, cause and consequence in one clear chain.
2. Separate current exposure from possible future impact.
3. Show why existing controls or authority are insufficient.
4. Compare intervention options by impact, speed and residual exposure.
5. Recommend an action and specify the latest safe decision point.
6. Define containment, monitoring and de-escalation conditions.

## Output format
Use: Escalation Summary, Current Exposure, Evidence, Controls to Date, Options, Recommendation, Decision Required and Monitoring Plan.

## Quality checks
- Severity follows the agreed risk framework.
- Evidence and assumptions are clearly separated.
- The requested intervention is within the recipient's authority.
