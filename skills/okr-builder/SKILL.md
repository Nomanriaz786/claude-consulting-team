# OKR Builder

## When to use it
Use at the start of a quarter or planning cycle, after a strategy is agreed, or when teams are busy but outcomes are unclear.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Agreed strategy, priorities or roadmap
- Current performance data and baselines
- Team structure and capacity
- Existing goals or targets

## Method
1. Choose no more than three to five objectives that link directly to stated strategic priorities.
2. Write two to four key results per objective, each with a baseline, target and date.
3. Separate outcome key results from activity tasks, and move tasks into initiatives.
4. Assign one accountable owner per objective and per key result.
5. Check cross-team dependencies and capacity, and cut goals that cannot be resourced.
6. Set a weekly check-in format, a mid-quarter review and an end-of-quarter scoring rule.

## Output format
Provide an OKR table (objective, key result, baseline, target, owner, data source), linked initiatives, dependency notes and the review calendar.

## Quality checks
- Every key result can be measured from an existing or planned data source
- Every objective links to a stated strategic priority
- Total commitments fit stated team capacity
