# Technology Due Diligence

## When to use it
Use during acquisition, investment or partnership evaluation before commitments and integration assumptions are fixed.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background and deal thesis
- Technology, application, data and vendor inventories
- Costs, contracts, architecture and delivery plans
- Security, privacy, resilience and incident evidence

## Method
1. Convert the deal thesis into technology diligence questions and materiality thresholds.
2. Assess architecture, product capability, estate health, data and delivery organisation.
3. Review ownership, licences, contracts, security, privacy and continuity evidence.
4. Identify technical debt, key-person reliance, concentration and hidden commitments.
5. Estimate separation, integration, remediation and ongoing operating implications.
6. Classify findings as deal issue, condition, valuation input or post-close action.

## Output format
Provide an executive finding set, red-flag register, evidence gaps and integration implications. Use columns for finding, evidence, impact, confidence, action and timing.

## Quality checks
- Findings connect directly to the deal thesis and value assumptions.
- Evidence gaps are not treated as favourable evidence.
- Estimates state scope, assumptions, uncertainty and excluded items.
