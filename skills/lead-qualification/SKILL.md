# Lead Qualification

## When to use it
Use after initial contact or discovery when enough information exists to assess opportunity quality.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Call notes and correspondence
- Known need, stakeholders, timing and budget approach
- Your acceptance rules and delivery capacity

## Method
1. Summarise the business trigger and consequence of leaving it unresolved.
2. Test authority, need, urgency, funding path and decision process.
3. Distinguish confirmed evidence, buyer statements and unresolved assumptions.
4. Identify champion strength, stakeholder alignment and competitive alternatives.
5. Rate fit, win likelihood, delivery risk and opportunity cost separately.
6. Recommend advance, hold, nurture, refer or close with a dated next gate.

## Output format
Provide a qualification memo with evidence table, missing information, risk flags, decision and next gate.

## Quality checks
- Avoids treating enthusiasm as buying authority
- Includes a clear disqualification path
- Ties every rating to stated evidence
