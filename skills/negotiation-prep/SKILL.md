# Negotiation Prep

## When to use it
Use before proposal discussions, procurement meetings or contract negotiations.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Current proposal and statement of work
- Known client concerns and stakeholder roles
- Your priorities, limits and alternatives

## Method
1. Define must-haves, preferences, walk-away points and best alternative.
2. Infer client interests from evidence and label every inference.
3. Build a concession ladder with reciprocal trades, never isolated giveaways.
4. Prepare responses for fee, scope, timing, risk and term objections.
5. Model scenarios and their effect on effort, margin and exposure.
6. Set meeting roles, pause points, approval limits and documentation steps.

## Output format
Provide an interest map, issue table, concession ladder, scenario table, talk track and approval limits.

## Quality checks
- Links each concession to a reciprocal benefit
- Protects explicit walk-away conditions
- Does not misrepresent authority or alternatives
