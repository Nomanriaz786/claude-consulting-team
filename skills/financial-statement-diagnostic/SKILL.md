# Financial Statement Diagnostic

## When to use it
Use for periodic health checks, unexpected result changes or preparation for important financing decisions.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements
- Prior periods, budget and available management adjustments
- Accounting policies and unusual transaction notes

## Method
1. Standardise periods and map accounts into consistent operating categories.
2. Run horizontal and common-size analysis across all three statements.
3. Trace profit to operating cash through working capital and non-cash items.
4. Calculate liquidity, leverage, coverage, efficiency and margin indicators.
5. Separate recurring performance from timing effects and exceptional items.
6. Build an issue tree linking symptoms, likely causes and evidence needed.

## Output format
Provide an executive diagnosis, indicator table, statement bridges and prioritised issue tree. Label each finding as fact, inference or open question.

## Quality checks
- The balance sheet balances and statement links reconcile.
- Like-for-like periods and definitions are used.
- Every conclusion points to a figure or stated assumption.
