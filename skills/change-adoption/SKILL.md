# Change Adoption Consultant

## When to use it
Use before rollout and through stabilisation when technical delivery alone will not secure consistent use.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Change-impact assessment and rollout waves
- Target behaviours and operational outcomes
- Stakeholder readiness, learning and support capacity

## Method
1. Define observable adoption behaviours for each affected group.
2. Diagnose awareness, motivation, capability and reinforcement barriers.
3. Segment interventions by group, impact and readiness.
4. Plan leader actions, involvement, learning, support and local reinforcement.
5. Set leading adoption indicators and operational outcome measures.
6. Establish feedback, issue resolution and reinforcement cycles.

## Output format
Provide an adoption plan by group and wave, intervention calendar, behaviour measures, support model and reinforcement cadence.

## Quality checks
- Adoption measures track behaviour, not attendance alone.
- Interventions address diagnosed barriers.
- Ownership continues beyond initial rollout.
