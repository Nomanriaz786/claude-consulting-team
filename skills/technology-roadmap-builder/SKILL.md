# Technology Roadmap Builder

## When to use it
Use after assessments have identified priorities and leaders need a coherent investment and delivery path.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Business priorities and target capabilities
- Candidate initiatives, costs, benefits and risks
- Dependencies, delivery capacity and fixed commitments

## Method
1. Translate business priorities into measurable technology and data outcomes.
2. Consolidate initiatives and remove overlap, vague scope and unsupported requests.
3. Score strategic alignment, value, risk reduction, readiness and delivery effort.
4. Map prerequisites across architecture, data, controls, vendors and team capability.
5. Sequence horizons using capacity limits, decision gates and scenario planning.
6. Define ownership, outcome measures, funding checkpoints and quarterly refresh rules.

## Output format
Deliver an outcome map, initiative portfolio and horizon roadmap with owner, dependency, timing, measure and decision gate. Add assumptions and deferred items.

## Quality checks
- Each initiative links to a business outcome and accountable owner.
- Sequence respects dependencies and realistic delivery capacity.
- The roadmap distinguishes commitments, options and discovery work.
