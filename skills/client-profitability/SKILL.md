# Client Profitability

## When to use it
Use when high-revenue accounts feel costly, service intensity varies or account tiers need redesign.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Revenue, discounts and credits by client
- Delivery, support and account-management activity data
- Direct labour rates and third-party costs
- Shared-cost pools and client attributes

## Method
1. Define client and account groupings, then reconcile revenue totals.
2. Assign direct delivery and third-party costs to each relationship.
3. Use time-driven activity-based costing for support and account effort.
4. Calculate contribution before and after decision-relevant service costs.
5. Segment clients by contribution, growth, payment behaviour and service intensity.
6. Set retain, reprice, redesign or exit actions with relationship risks.

## Output format
Provide a client profitability table, segment matrix and account action list. Show revenue, service cost, contribution, key driver and proposed action.

## Quality checks
- Client totals reconcile to the source accounts.
- Unrecorded service effort is identified as an estimate.
- Exit choices include stranded cost and concentration risk.
