# Growth Options

## When to use it
Use when current growth has slowed, a new ambition is set or strategic alternatives are too narrow.

## What Claude needs
- Growth ambition and time horizon
- Current model from client-context.md
- Customer, market and competitor evidence
- Capabilities, constraints and risk appetite

## Method
1. Establish the growth baseline, objective and binding constraints.
2. Generate options across existing and new customers, offers, channels and markets.
3. Use the Ansoff matrix to expose gaps without treating it as the final answer.
4. Describe each option's value logic, required capabilities and key assumptions.
5. Compare options for attractiveness, fit, feasibility, risk and reversibility.
6. Build a balanced portfolio of core, adjacent and exploratory moves.

## Output format
Present a growth option map, option cards, comparison matrix, portfolio recommendation and critical assumptions.

## Quality checks
- Options are materially distinct and strategically coherent
- Growth logic identifies who changes which behaviour
- Capability and execution constraints affect the recommendation
