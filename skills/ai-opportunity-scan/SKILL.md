# AI Opportunity Scan

## When to use it
Use before an AI programme, workshop or investment decision to locate valuable, feasible and responsible use cases.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- A list of workflows, roles and recurring decisions
- Current pain points, volumes and service expectations
- Data sources, technology constraints and risk boundaries

## Method
1. Segment work into create, classify, extract, predict, decide and interact activities.
2. Identify friction, judgement load, repeatability and data availability for each activity.
3. Frame each opportunity with user, trigger, task, input, output and human oversight.
4. Score value, feasibility, data readiness and harm exposure on defined scales.
5. Place candidates on an impact-effort matrix and flag dependencies or controls.
6. Select pilots with hypotheses, success measures, owners and stop conditions.

## Output format
Provide an opportunity table with use case, user, problem, AI task, value, feasibility, risks and priority. Follow with pilot briefs and a do-not-pursue list.

## Quality checks
- Every use case begins with a verified business problem.
- Scores use consistent definitions and cite available evidence.
- Human oversight and failure consequences are explicit.
