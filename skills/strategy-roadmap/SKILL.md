# Strategy Roadmap

## When to use it
Use after strategic direction is agreed and before detailed functional planning begins.

## What Claude needs
- Chosen strategy and intended outcomes
- Context and constraints from client-context.md
- Key initiatives, assumptions and capabilities
- Decision owners and planning horizon

## Method
1. Translate the strategy into a small set of measurable strategic outcomes.
2. Identify initiatives, capability enablers and experiments required for each outcome.
3. Map dependencies, constraints and critical assumptions across the work.
4. Sequence actions into horizons based on value, readiness and learning needs.
5. Define milestones, decision gates, owners and evidence requirements.
6. Check coherence against stated choices and remove activity without strategic linkage.

## Output format
Provide strategic outcomes, a horizon roadmap, initiative cards, dependency map, decision gates and an ownership table.

## Quality checks
- Every initiative traces to a strategic outcome
- Sequence reflects dependencies and evidence needs
- Milestones describe outcomes or decisions, not activity alone
