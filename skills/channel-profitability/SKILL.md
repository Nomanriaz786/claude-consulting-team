# Channel Profitability

## When to use it
Use before shifting sales investment, adding a channel or changing channel incentives and service models.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Revenue, orders and discounts by channel
- Acquisition, sales, fulfilment, return and support costs
- Channel partner fees and incentive terms
- Customer overlap and attribution rules

## Method
1. Define channel boundaries and a consistent order or customer unit.
2. Reconcile channel revenue, discounts, refunds and partner deductions.
3. Assign direct acquisition, selling, fulfilment and support costs.
4. Resolve shared journeys with explicit first-touch, last-touch or blended attribution.
5. Compare contribution, cash timing, scalability and concentration by channel.
6. Test investment shifts while accounting for cannibalisation and capacity.

## Output format
Provide a channel economics table, attribution assumptions and investment scenarios. Show revenue, full variable cost, contribution, cash lag and constraint.

## Quality checks
- Channel totals reconcile without duplicate revenue.
- Attribution choices are explicit and sensitivity-tested.
- Partner deductions and returns are included in realised economics.
