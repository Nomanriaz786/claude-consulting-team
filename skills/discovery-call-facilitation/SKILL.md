# Discovery Call Facilitation

## When to use it
Use immediately before or during a client discovery call to guide timing, listening and next steps.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Meeting length, attendees and objective
- Discovery brief and question set
- Known sensitivities and desired next gate

## Method
1. Open with purpose, time, roles, confidentiality and the client's desired outcome.
2. Use a funnel from broad context through trigger, impact, priority and constraints.
3. Capture exact facts, examples, measures and ownership without solving too early.
4. Test understanding with concise playback at topic transitions.
5. Surface decision process, alternatives, timing and funding path directly.
6. Close with agreed facts, open questions, owners, dates and next decision.

## Output format
Provide a timed run sheet with facilitator prompts, listening cues, parking lot and closing checklist.

## Quality checks
- Protects enough time for impact and decision process
- Uses playback without adding unsupported interpretation
- Ends with explicit owners and dates
