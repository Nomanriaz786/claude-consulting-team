# Case Study Writer

## When to use it
Use after an engagement closes with measurable results and the client has agreed to be referenced, or when building a library of proof for a target segment.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Engagement brief, starting situation and baseline numbers
- Deliverables, outcomes and dates
- Client quotes and what the client has approved for publication
- Target audience for the case study

## Method
1. Confirm what the client has approved: name, logo, figures, quotes, or anonymised only.
2. Describe the starting problem in the client's own terms, including what it was costing them.
3. Explain the approach as three to five key decisions or steps, not a list of activities.
4. Quantify results against the baseline, stating the period and how each figure was measured.
5. Choose one quote that names a result or a change in confidence, and mark it for client sign-off.
6. Add a short line on who this is relevant for and a clear next step for the reader.

## Output format
Provide a one-page case study with headline result, situation, approach, results table (metric, before, after, period, source), quote and call to action, plus a 60-word summary for proposals and social posts.

## Quality checks
- Every number traces to a named source and baseline
- Nothing goes beyond what the client approved for publication
- Credits other contributing factors fairly instead of claiming all of the result
