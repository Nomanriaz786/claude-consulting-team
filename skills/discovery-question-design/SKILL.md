# Discovery Question Design

## When to use it
Use before discovery interviews, workshops or follow-up meetings with client stakeholders.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Meeting purpose, attendees and available time
- Existing hypotheses and known facts
- Decisions the conversation must enable

## Method
1. Convert each required decision into an evidence need.
2. Group questions by context, trigger, impact, current response, desired outcome and buying process.
3. Write neutral open questions before diagnostic or confirming questions.
4. Add probes for examples, frequency, consequence, ownership and evidence.
5. Prioritise must-ask questions and create optional branches by answer type.
6. Test wording for bias, duplication, jargon and time demand.

## Output format
Provide a sequenced question guide with purpose, primary question, probes, evidence sought and optional branch.

## Quality checks
- Each question serves a stated decision
- Questions do not embed the preferred answer
- The must-ask set fits the available time
