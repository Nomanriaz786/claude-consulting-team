# Prospect Fit Screen

## When to use it
Use before spending significant time on outreach, tailoring or meetings with a specific prospect.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Ideal client profile and exclusion rules
- Public or supplied facts about the prospect
- Capacity, timing and conflict constraints

## Method
1. Build a fact base using only supplied or attributable information.
2. Test the prospect against need, timing, access, authority, value and delivery fit.
3. Apply hard exclusions for ethics, conflicts, capability or capacity.
4. Rate evidence confidence and expose important unknowns.
5. Classify the prospect as pursue, nurture, refer or decline.
6. Recommend the smallest next action that could resolve uncertainty.

## Output format
Provide a fit scorecard with criterion, evidence, confidence and result, followed by decision, rationale and next action.

## Quality checks
- Does not infer private facts from weak signals
- Applies the same criteria used for other prospects
- Makes uncertainty visible in the final decision
