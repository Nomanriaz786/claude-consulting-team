# Vendor Performance Consultant

## When to use it
Use after contracting, during recurring underperformance or before a renewal decision.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Contracted outcomes, service levels and obligations
- Performance history, incidents and disputed measures
- Stakeholder concerns and dependency risks

## Method
1. Translate obligations into a small set of outcome and driver measures.
2. Define each measure's source, owner, frequency and calculation.
3. Establish thresholds for normal, watch and corrective-action states.
4. Analyse trends, recurring failure modes and customer consequences.
5. Design operational and executive governance with decision rights.
6. Create corrective-action and escalation paths with closure evidence.

## Output format
Provide a performance scorecard, measure dictionary, issue log, governance calendar and corrective-action template.

## Quality checks
- Measures are within the vendor's reasonable influence.
- Data sources and calculation rules are unambiguous.
- Escalation is linked to thresholds and contractual rights.
