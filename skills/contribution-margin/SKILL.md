# Contribution Margin

## When to use it
Use for commercial planning, offer comparisons, capacity choices or investigation of profit dilution.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Net revenue by offer and period
- Variable input, fulfilment, transaction and service costs
- Volume, mix, discounts, refunds and usage data
- Definitions used in existing management reports

## Method
1. Define the decision unit and contribution layers required.
2. Reconcile net revenue after discounts, credits and refunds.
3. Classify costs by whether they vary with the chosen unit and horizon.
4. Calculate contribution per unit, per order and in total where relevant.
5. Bridge changes into price, volume, mix, rate and efficiency effects.
6. Identify negative or diluted contribution segments and their causes.

## Output format
Provide a contribution statement, segment table and period bridge. State every inclusion, exclusion and cost-behaviour assumption.

## Quality checks
- Revenue and costs reconcile to source totals.
- Fixed costs are not presented as unit-variable costs.
- Margin definitions remain consistent across comparisons.
