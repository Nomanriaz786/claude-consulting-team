# Status Briefing

## When to use it
Use ahead of reviews, calls or governance meetings where a rapid and reliable position is required.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and approved baseline
- Current milestones, outcomes and delivery evidence
- Risks, issues, dependencies and actions
- Audience, briefing purpose and time available

## Method
1. Compare current position with the last approved baseline.
2. Identify material progress, variance and changes since the prior briefing.
3. Diagnose root causes and distinguish risk from realised issue.
4. Rank exceptions by impact and urgency using agreed thresholds.
5. State corrective action, accountable owner and expected timing.
6. Highlight decisions or support needed from the audience.

## Output format
Use: Overall Position, Progress Since Last Update, Key Exceptions, Recovery Actions, Decisions Needed and Next Milestones. Add a compact status table.

## Quality checks
- Claims are current as of a stated data date.
- Overall status matches milestone and risk evidence.
- Each exception has an owner and response.
