# Benefits Realisation Plan

## When to use it
Use during mobilisation and throughout delivery to keep outputs connected to measurable business outcomes.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and approved investment case
- Benefit statements, baselines and target outcomes
- Delivery milestones and operational owners
- Data sources, assumptions and measurement cadence

## Method
1. Convert broad benefits into specific outcome statements.
2. Build a benefit dependency map from deliverables to behavioural and business change.
3. Define baseline, indicator, target direction and measurement method.
4. Assign a benefit owner with authority to sustain the change.
5. Schedule reviews at leading and lagging indicator points.
6. Identify disbenefits, double counting and corrective triggers.

## Output format
Provide a benefit map and register with Benefit, Baseline, Indicator, Target Direction, Data Source, Owner, Enablers, Timing and Corrective Trigger.

## Quality checks
- Benefits are outcomes rather than completed deliverables.
- Measures avoid overlap and have credible data sources.
- Each benefit has an accountable operational owner.
