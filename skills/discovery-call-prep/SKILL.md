# Discovery Call Prep

## When to use it
Use before an initial or follow-up discovery call to enter with clear hypotheses, questions and decisions to make.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Prospect organisation, attendees and meeting length
- Known trigger, goals and prior correspondence
- Your offer, relevant experience and constraints

## Method
1. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions and unknowns in a three-column evidence log.
2. Form three testable hypotheses about the trigger, impact and decision process.
3. Map each attendee's likely role, interest, influence and possible concern.
4. Build a timed agenda covering context, needs, impact, fit, process and next step.
5. Draft prioritised questions with follow-up probes and evidence sought.
6. Define call success, warning signs and a suitable closing path.

## Output format
Provide a one-page brief with situation, attendees, hypotheses, timed agenda, question bank, warning signs and closing options.

## Quality checks
- Labels every assumption and avoids presenting it as fact
- Fits the agenda within the stated meeting length
- Uses open questions before solution discussion
