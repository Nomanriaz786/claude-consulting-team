# Compensation Bands

## When to use it
Use when pay has grown case by case, before a hiring push, after staff raise fairness concerns, or when introducing levels and promotions.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Current roles, levels and pay data
- Market pay data sources
- Budget available for pay changes
- Pay philosophy, such as the target market position

## Method
1. Group roles into job families and define levels with clear expectations of scope and impact.
2. Set the target market position, such as the median, and record the data sources used.
3. Build pay bands with a minimum, midpoint and maximum for each level.
4. Place current staff in the bands and flag anyone below the minimum or above the maximum.
5. Check for unexplained pay gaps between comparable roles and groups.
6. Cost the adjustments, then define promotion criteria, review timing and how pay decisions will be explained.

## Output format
Provide a level framework, band table, current placement analysis, adjustment cost, promotion criteria and a communication plan.

## Quality checks
- Bands rest on cited market data
- Unexplained pay gaps are flagged for review
- Employment law and equal pay questions are flagged for qualified advice
