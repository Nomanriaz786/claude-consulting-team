# Vendor Risk Assessment

## When to use it
Use before contracting or renewal, after a material service change, or when provider concentration becomes concerning.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Service scope, information access and integration details
- Due diligence evidence, contract terms and service history
- Criticality criteria, alternatives and internal owner

## Method
1. Tier the provider by service criticality, information sensitivity and substitutability.
2. Map delivery model, locations, subcontractors, access and dependency chain.
3. Assess security, privacy, resilience, financial viability and operational controls.
4. Compare evidence and contract protections with risks and required safeguards.
5. Model disruption, breach, degradation and exit scenarios.
6. Recommend accept, mitigate, renegotiate, replace or monitor actions with triggers.

## Output format
Provide a risk scorecard, evidence gaps, contract safeguards and action plan with owner and date. Include monitoring indicators and an exit-readiness summary.

## Quality checks
- Provider claims are separated from independently supported evidence.
- Subcontractor and concentration risks are included.
- Exit feasibility covers data return, deletion and service transition.
