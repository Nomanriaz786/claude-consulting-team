# Workplan Designer

## When to use it
Use after scope agreement to plan delivery or provide the backbone of a proposal.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Available people, dates and capacity
- Client inputs, approvals and dependencies

## Method
1. Decompose each workstream into decision-oriented activities and outputs.
2. Sequence tasks by dependency, information flow and review needs.
3. Mark milestones, decision gates and critical client inputs.
4. Assign responsible, accountable, consulted and informed roles with a RACI.
5. Estimate effort as ranges and expose capacity conflicts.
6. Run a pre-mortem and add mitigations or contingency points.

## Output format
Provide a phased workplan with activity, owner, timing, dependency, output and gate, plus RACI and key risks.

## Quality checks
- Dates respect all stated dependencies
- Client effort is visible rather than assumed
- Each phase ends in an output or decision
