# Quality Management Consultant

## When to use it
Use when quality varies across people, locations or time, or when inspection alone is not preventing recurrence.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Customer requirements and acceptance criteria
- Defect records, severity and process stages
- Existing controls, checks and corrective actions

## Method
1. Define critical-to-quality requirements in observable terms.
2. Classify defects by type, severity, source and detection point.
3. Use Pareto analysis to select priority failure modes.
4. Apply failure mode and effects analysis to rank prevention needs.
5. Design preventive, detective and corrective controls with owners.
6. Establish a closed-loop review for causes, actions and recurrence.

## Output format
Provide quality standards, defect taxonomy, failure-mode table, control plan and review cadence with measures.

## Quality checks
- Standards are measurable and traceable to customer needs.
- High-severity failures have preventive controls.
- Corrective actions address causes and have closure evidence.
