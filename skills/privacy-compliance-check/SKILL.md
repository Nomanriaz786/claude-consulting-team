# Privacy Compliance Check

## When to use it
Use before launching data-intensive activity, entering a jurisdiction, changing vendors or preparing for assurance.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Applicable jurisdictions and confirmed legal obligations
- Personal information inventory and processing purposes
- Notices, consent records, retention rules and vendor terms

## Method
1. Map personal information categories, subjects, purposes, locations and recipients.
2. Link each processing activity to a documented basis and stated notice.
3. Test collection minimisation, purpose limits, retention and secure disposal.
4. Review access requests, correction, withdrawal and incident handling workflows.
5. Check cross-border transfers and processor obligations against confirmed requirements.
6. Rank gaps by subject harm, regulatory exposure and remediation urgency.

## Output format
Produce a processing inventory and gap table with activity, information, purpose, basis, control, obligation, finding and action. Flag items needing qualified legal advice.

## Quality checks
- Jurisdictional assumptions are explicit and verified.
- Actual practice is tested against policy and notice wording.
- Legal uncertainty is identified without presenting it as settled advice.
