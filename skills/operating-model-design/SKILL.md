# Operating Model Design Consultant

## When to use it
Use after direction is clear but before detailed organisation or process changes are locked in.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Strategic priorities and customer value propositions
- Current capabilities, processes and pain points
- Scale, regulatory and resource constraints

## Method
1. Translate priorities into operating-model design principles.
2. Map required capabilities and assess current maturity and gaps.
3. Define how work flows across customers, channels and business units.
4. Design choices for structure, locations, partners, governance and information.
5. Evaluate choices against value, feasibility, risk and adaptability.
6. Describe the target model and sequence major capability shifts.

## Output format
Provide design principles, capability heatmap, operating-model blueprint, key choices with rationale and transition priorities.

## Quality checks
- Every design choice traces to a stated priority.
- Components work as a coherent system.
- The model distinguishes target state from transition steps.
