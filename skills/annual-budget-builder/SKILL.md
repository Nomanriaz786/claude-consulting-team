# Annual Budget Builder

## When to use it
Use in the planning season before a financial year, after a major change in strategy, or when the current budget no longer reflects reality.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Prior-year actuals by month
- Strategic priorities and targets
- Headcount plan and pay assumptions
- Known contracts, price changes and planned investments

## Method
1. Agree the budget structure: periods, cost centres, owners and approval steps.
2. Set revenue drivers such as volume, price, conversion, churn and new customers, and document each assumption.
3. Build costs from drivers: headcount by role and start month, variable costs as a share of revenue, fixed costs by contract.
4. Add planned investments with timing and expected payback.
5. Produce monthly profit, cash and headcount views, and test one downside and one upside case.
6. Review with budget owners and record agreed changes and open disagreements.

## Output format
Provide a monthly budget with driver sheet, assumptions log, profit and loss, cash view, headcount plan, scenario comparison and owner sign-off list.

## Quality checks
- Every line has a driver or a documented basis
- Shows the cash impact, not just profit
- Totals reconcile across monthly, annual and owner views
