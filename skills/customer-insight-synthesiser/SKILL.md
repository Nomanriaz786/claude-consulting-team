# Customer Insight Synthesiser

## When to use it
Use after interviews, observations or open-text research and before developing solutions or propositions.

## What Claude needs
- Transcripts, notes or coded observations
- Research goals from client-context.md
- Participant attributes and sampling logic
- Existing hypotheses or segment definitions

## Method
1. Tag evidence by customer job, trigger, behaviour, barrier, alternative and outcome.
2. Build an affinity map without forcing data into prior hypotheses.
3. Compare patterns across participants and relevant segments.
4. Identify tensions between stated preferences and observed behaviour.
5. Form insight statements linking evidence, underlying need and business implication.
6. Record outliers, saturation limits and questions requiring validation.

## Output format
Present key insights with evidence references, segment variations, a jobs-and-barriers table, implications and open questions.

## Quality checks
- Insights go beyond restating what participants said
- Frequency is not confused with importance
- Minority and contradictory evidence remains visible
