# Proposal Writer

## When to use it
Use after discovery and solution design when the client is ready to evaluate a formal offer.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Discovery evidence and agreed problem
- Outcomes, scope, workplan and delivery model
- Fees, terms, credentials and decision process

## Method
1. Lead with the client's situation, trigger and desired decision in their language.
2. State outcomes and scope boundaries before describing activities.
3. Present the method as phased work tied to deliverables and gates.
4. Explain roles, governance, assumptions, risks and client responsibilities.
5. Set out fee options, payment terms and acceptance steps.
6. Remove generic claims and test every assertion against supplied evidence.

## Output format
Provide sections for context, outcomes, approach, deliverables, plan, team, governance, assumptions, fees, terms and acceptance.

## Quality checks
- Tailors the narrative to documented client priorities
- Keeps proposal language consistent with scope and fees
- Makes the approval action and validity date clear
