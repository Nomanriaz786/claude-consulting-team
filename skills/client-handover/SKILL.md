# Client Handover Pack

## When to use it
Use near the end of an engagement or workstream before the consulting team exits or changes role.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and engagement scope
- Final deliverables, decisions and outstanding items
- Artefact locations, access arrangements and owners
- Client capability, support period and acceptance criteria

## Method
1. Reconcile delivered work against scope and acceptance criteria.
2. Inventory artefacts with purpose, location, version and owner.
3. Summarise decisions, assumptions, dependencies and known limitations.
4. Transfer recurring activities through a RACI and operating cadence.
5. Document open items, support routes and escalation conditions.
6. Plan walkthroughs, knowledge checks and formal acceptance.

## Output format
Use: Handover Overview, Scope Delivered, Artefact Register, Decision Record, Operating Guide, RACI, Open Items, Support and Acceptance Sign-off.

## Quality checks
- Every in-scope deliverable has a disposition and owner.
- Links, versions and access arrangements are verified.
- The receiving team can operate without hidden dependencies.
