# Implementation Roadmap

## When to use it
Use after direction is agreed but before detailed delivery planning begins.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and approved recommendation
- Target outcomes, scope and constraints
- Capabilities, dependencies and available owners
- Known milestones, risks and decision gates

## Method
1. Translate target outcomes into measurable end-state capabilities.
2. Define workstreams using a MECE breakdown of required change.
3. Sequence foundations, pilots, rollout and stabilisation by dependency.
4. Mark milestones, decision gates and critical cross-workstream handoffs.
5. Assign accountable owners and indicative resource needs.
6. Test the roadmap through dependency mapping and a delivery pre-mortem.

## Output format
Provide Roadmap Principles, Phases, Workstream Table, Milestone Timeline, Dependencies, Decision Gates, Ownership and First Actions.

## Quality checks
- Every activity links to an intended outcome.
- Dependencies and gates are sequenced logically.
- Near-term actions are more detailed than later phases.
