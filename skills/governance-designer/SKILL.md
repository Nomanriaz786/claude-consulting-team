# Governance Designer

## When to use it
Use at mobilisation or when an active engagement suffers slow decisions or unclear accountability.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Statement of work, workplan and stakeholder map
- Decision types and named role holders
- Reporting, risk and escalation expectations

## Method
1. List recurring and milestone decisions required for delivery.
2. Assign propose, input, decide and execute rights for each decision type.
3. Design meeting cadence, purpose, attendees, inputs and outputs.
4. Create status, risk, issue, decision and change-control registers.
5. Define escalation thresholds, route, response time and resolution owner.
6. Test governance load against engagement scale and simplify duplication.

## Output format
Provide a governance map, decision-rights table, meeting calendar, register definitions and escalation path.

## Quality checks
- Gives every material decision one clear decider
- Separates risks, issues, decisions and changes
- Keeps governance proportionate to engagement complexity
