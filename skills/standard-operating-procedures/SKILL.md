# Standard Operating Procedures Consultant

## When to use it
Use for stable, recurring activities where variation creates risk or rework. Do not document a broken process before redesigning it.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Approved process map and accountable owner
- Required inputs, systems, controls and outputs
- Common exceptions and escalation paths

## Method
1. Define scope, audience, prerequisites and completion standard.
2. Convert the process into concise action-led instructions.
3. Add decision criteria, control points and evidence requirements.
4. Document exceptions, escalation triggers and recovery steps.
5. Test the procedure against routine and edge-case scenarios.
6. Set version ownership, approval and review frequency.

## Output format
Provide purpose, scope, responsibilities, prerequisites, numbered procedure, exception table, completion checklist and document-control block.

## Quality checks
- A trained reader can act without hidden knowledge.
- Steps match the approved process and role authority.
- Safety, control and escalation points are unambiguous.
