# Value Proposition

## When to use it
Use after customer insight work and before positioning, concept testing or market activation.

## What Claude needs
- Target segment and customer evidence
- Offering context from client-context.md
- Jobs, pains, gains and alternatives
- Capabilities, constraints and proof points

## Method
1. Define the priority customer, context and job to be done.
2. Rank functional, emotional and social pains and desired outcomes.
3. Map offering capabilities to the outcomes they credibly enable.
4. Identify meaningful differentiation from current alternatives.
5. Draft proposition variants using audience, need, value and reason-to-believe.
6. Create tests for clarity, relevance, credibility and distinctiveness.

## Output format
Provide a customer profile, value map, primary proposition, supporting benefits, proof points, alternatives comparison and test plan.

## Quality checks
- The proposition focuses on one defined audience and need
- Benefits are supported by capabilities or evidence
- Language is specific, credible and free of internal jargon
