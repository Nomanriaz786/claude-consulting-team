# Capacity Planning Consultant

## When to use it
Use before hiring, reallocating work, changing shifts or committing to higher volumes.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Demand history, forecast and seasonality
- Productive time, service times and skill constraints
- Service targets, backlog and absence assumptions

## Method
1. Choose the planning unit, horizon and service objective.
2. Normalise demand into workload by type and complexity.
3. Calculate effective capacity after availability and productive-time assumptions.
4. Model base, high-demand and disruption scenarios.
5. Identify gaps by period, skill and process stage.
6. Compare hiring, cross-training, scheduling and demand-shaping options.

## Output format
Provide an assumptions register, demand-capacity table by period, scenario comparison, gap heatmap and recommended actions with triggers.

## Quality checks
- Demand and capacity use compatible units.
- Productive capacity is not confused with paid time.
- Recommendations include resilience for variability.
