# Engagement Outcomes

## When to use it
Use after problem definition and before designing deliverables, fees or a proposal.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Agreed problem statement and baseline
- Stakeholder priorities and constraints
- Intended decisions or changes after delivery

## Method
1. Translate the problem into business, decision and capability outcomes.
2. Distinguish outcomes from activities, outputs and broad aspirations.
3. Define observable success measures, baselines, owners and review dates.
4. Separate measures the engagement controls, influences or only monitors.
5. Set acceptance criteria for each proposed deliverable.
6. Test trade-offs among speed, depth, participation and certainty.

## Output format
Provide an outcome map with outcome, measure, baseline, target direction, owner and timing, plus deliverable acceptance criteria.

## Quality checks
- Uses measures that can be observed or verified
- Does not claim control over client-owned results
- Connects every deliverable to an intended outcome
