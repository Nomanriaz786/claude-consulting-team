# Budget Variance Analyst

## When to use it
Use after a reporting period or when a forecast has moved materially away from plan.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Actual, budget and prior-period results
- Budget assumptions and operational driver data
- Account mappings, accrual notes and exceptional items

## Method
1. Align account definitions, periods and signs across all data sets.
2. Rank absolute variances and isolate the items driving most of the gap.
3. Decompose revenue into volume, mix and price effects.
4. Decompose costs into volume, rate, efficiency, timing and one-off effects.
5. Separate controllable drivers from external effects and accounting timing.
6. Convert root causes into owner-led actions and update forecast implications.

## Output format
Provide a variance waterfall, driver table and action register. Show actual, budget, variance amount, cause, owner and forecast effect.

## Quality checks
- Detailed variances sum to the reported total.
- Volume, mix and price effects use one consistent method.
- Explanations identify causes rather than restating variances.
