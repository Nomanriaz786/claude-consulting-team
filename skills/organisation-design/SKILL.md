# Organisation Design Consultant

## When to use it
Use after capabilities and workflows are understood, especially during growth, consolidation or a shift in operating model.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Target capabilities and core workflows
- Current structure, roles, headcount and locations
- Design constraints and leadership accountabilities

## Method
1. Set design principles based on work and customer outcomes.
2. Group activities by capability, interdependence and coordination need.
3. Compare functional, product, geographic and hybrid archetypes.
4. Design units, reporting lines, layers and manageable spans.
5. Define critical interfaces and mechanisms for cross-unit work.
6. Test the design against growth, absence and conflict scenarios.

## Output format
Provide design principles, option comparison, proposed structure, unit mandates, span analysis and key interface map.

## Quality checks
- Structure follows work rather than incumbent preferences.
- Accountabilities have no material gaps or duplicate ownership.
- The design is feasible within stated constraints.
