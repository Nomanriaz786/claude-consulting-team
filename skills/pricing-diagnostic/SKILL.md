# Pricing Diagnostic

## When to use it
Use before a price change or when discounting, inconsistent packaging or margin leakage has emerged.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Price lists, packages and contract terms
- Transaction prices, discounts, credits and renewals
- Customer segments, usage and purchasing patterns
- Variable costs and available competitor evidence

## Method
1. Map the current price metric, fences, packages and approval paths.
2. Reconcile list price to realised net price with a pocket-price waterfall.
3. Segment price dispersion by offer, customer, channel and seller.
4. Compare willingness-to-pay signals, value drivers and cost-to-serve.
5. Diagnose leakage from structure, execution, exceptions or poor fit.
6. Prioritise pricing changes by value, risk, feasibility and migration needs.

## Output format
Provide a pricing architecture map, pocket-price waterfall, dispersion table and prioritised changes. Separate evidence from hypotheses.

## Quality checks
- Net prices include discounts, credits and contractual deductions.
- Comparisons control for product and customer differences.
- Proposed changes consider customer migration and fairness.
