# Market Sizer

## When to use it
Use for market entry, investment prioritisation or planning when headline market figures are not decision-ready.

## What Claude needs
- Market definition and customer segments
- Business context from client-context.md
- Volume, spend, adoption or usage evidence
- Geographic scope and time horizon
- Known capacity and channel constraints

## Method
1. Define total, serviceable and realistically obtainable market boundaries.
2. Select top-down and bottom-up sizing approaches with compatible units.
3. Build driver equations and document every assumption and source.
4. Reconcile estimates, investigating material gaps rather than averaging them.
5. Run low, base and high cases on the most sensitive assumptions.
6. Translate market size into implications for capacity, channels and timing.

## Output format
Provide definition boxes, calculation tables, assumptions register, scenario range, sensitivity drivers and interpretation.

## Quality checks
- Units, periods and market boundaries remain consistent
- Estimates can be reproduced from stated inputs
- Reachable demand reflects real constraints and adoption timing
