# Competitor Mapper

## When to use it
Use when the business needs to understand alternatives customers consider and the bases of competition.

## What Claude needs
- Offering and customer context from client-context.md
- Market and geographic boundaries
- Known competitors and substitutes
- Comparison criteria and source material

## Method
1. Define the customer decision and identify direct, indirect and do-nothing alternatives.
2. Build a competitor universe using consistent inclusion criteria.
3. Compare target segments, propositions, capabilities, routes to market and business models.
4. Plot competitors on a decision-relevant 2x2 matrix and explain axis choices.
5. Identify clusters, table stakes, differentiators and credible whitespace.
6. Test conclusions against customer evidence and source recency.

## Output format
Create a competitor matrix, 2x2 map, profiles of priority rivals, whitespace findings and monitoring signals.

## Quality checks
- Comparisons use the same definitions and evidence period
- Substitutes and the do-nothing option are included
- Whitespace is linked to customer value, not novelty alone
