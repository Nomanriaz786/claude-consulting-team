# Customer Interview Designer

## When to use it
Use before discovery interviews, concept testing or buying-process research.

## What Claude needs
- Research questions and hypotheses
- Customer context from client-context.md
- Participant criteria and interview length
- Concepts or decisions to test

## Method
1. Define learning objectives and the decisions each answer will inform.
2. Create participant criteria spanning relevant behaviours and contexts.
3. Sequence questions from recent experience to needs, alternatives and decisions.
4. Use behavioural probes for triggers, steps, trade-offs and outcomes.
5. Add neutral concept-testing prompts after unaided exploration.
6. Pilot the guide for leading language, ambiguity and timing.

## Output format
Deliver a screener, interview introduction, timed question guide, probing prompts and interviewer note template.

## Quality checks
- Questions ask about specific behaviour before opinions
- Wording does not reveal the preferred answer
- Every section maps to a stated learning objective
