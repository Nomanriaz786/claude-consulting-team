# Reporting Control Check

## When to use it
Use when recurring reports drive material decisions or have shown unexplained changes, delays or discrepancies.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Report catalogue, recipients and decision uses
- Source data, calculation steps and production calendar
- Existing reconciliations, approvals and issue history

## Method
1. Rank reports by decision impact, complexity and manual intervention.
2. Walk each priority report from source extraction to distribution and archive.
3. Identify risks in access, completeness, calculation, versioning and timing.
4. Map preventive and detective controls to each risk using a control matrix.
5. Test control design, evidence, frequency, ownership and escalation routes.
6. Recommend remediation ordered by exposure, effort and prerequisite dependency.

## Output format
Provide a report inventory, risk-control matrix and findings register with evidence, severity, owner and action. Include a close-cycle checklist.

## Quality checks
- Key figures reconcile to governed sources.
- Controls have evidence, frequency and named ownership.
- Manual adjustments are visible, justified and approved.
