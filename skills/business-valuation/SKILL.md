# Business Valuation

## When to use it
Use before selling or buying a stake, negotiating with investors, settling a partner exit or planning succession.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Purpose and date of the valuation
- Three to five years of financial statements
- Forecast and its key assumptions
- Normalisation items such as owner pay or one-off costs
- Comparable transactions or listed peers where available

## Method
1. State the purpose, valuation date and the basis of value being estimated.
2. Normalise earnings for one-off items and non-market owner pay, documenting each adjustment.
3. Apply at least two methods, such as earnings multiples from comparables and discounted cash flow.
4. Document the discount rate, growth and multiple assumptions and the evidence behind each.
5. Run sensitivity on the two assumptions that move value most.
6. Reconcile the methods into a range and explain what would push value up or down.

## Output format
Provide a valuation summary with normalised earnings table, results by method, sensitivity table, final range, and key value drivers and risks.

## Quality checks
- Gives a range, not a single precise number
- Every adjustment and assumption is documented
- States clearly that it is an indicative estimate, not a formal or certified valuation
