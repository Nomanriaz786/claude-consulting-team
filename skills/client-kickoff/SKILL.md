# Client Kickoff

## When to use it
Use after signature and before substantive delivery work starts.

## What Claude needs
- `client-context.md` for business background
- Final statement of work and workplan
- Attendees, roles and stakeholder map
- Known risks, dependencies and required access

## Method
1. Translate contract commitments into a concise shared engagement charter.
2. Build an agenda covering outcomes, scope, approach, roles and ways of working.
3. Confirm governance, decision rights, escalation and change control.
4. Validate milestones, acceptance criteria, client inputs and access dates.
5. Facilitate a pre-mortem to surface delivery and relationship risks.
6. Capture decisions, actions, owners and dates in a kickoff record.

## Output format
Provide a timed agenda, engagement charter, decision-rights table, risk prompts and action register.

## Quality checks
- Matches the signed scope and latest workplan
- Gives each action one accountable owner
- Surfaces unresolved issues without silently changing terms
