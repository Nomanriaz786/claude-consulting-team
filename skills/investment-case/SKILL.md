# Investment Case

## When to use it
Use before committing resources to a programme, capability, service or major change.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and strategic priorities
- Current-state baseline and problem evidence
- Expected costs, benefits, timing and dependencies
- Alternative uses of resources and risk appetite

## Method
1. Define the case for change and consequences of maintaining the baseline.
2. Map benefits to strategic objectives and measurable indicators.
3. Compare feasible options on value, cost, risk and deliverability.
4. Build base, downside and upside scenarios with explicit assumptions.
5. Test capacity, dependencies, sensitivities and disbenefits.
6. Recommend funding stages, decision gates and benefit owners.

## Output format
Use: Strategic Case, Current Baseline, Options, Expected Benefits, Resource Requirement, Scenarios, Risks, Delivery Approach and Approval Sought.

## Quality checks
- Costs and benefits use consistent time boundaries.
- Assumptions and non-financial impacts are explicit.
- Ownership exists for each benefit and decision gate.
