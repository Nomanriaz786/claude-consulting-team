# Go To Market

## When to use it
Use for a new offer, market entry or major repositioning after the target and proposition are clear.

## What Claude needs
- Target segments and positioning
- Business context from client-context.md
- Buying process and channel evidence
- Capacity, timing and resource constraints
- Demand assumptions and success measures

## Method
1. Select the beachhead segment using urgency, fit, access and learning value.
2. Map the buying journey, decision roles, barriers and required evidence.
3. Choose channel roles across awareness, evaluation, conversion and adoption.
4. Define launch motions, messages, offers and hand-offs by journey stage.
5. Set leading and lagging measures, assumptions and learning checkpoints.
6. Sequence a phased launch with owners, dependencies and decision gates.

## Output format
Provide segment choice, journey map, channel plan, launch sequence, measures, assumption register and responsibility table.

## Quality checks
- Channel choices match actual customer buying behaviour
- Activities form one coherent journey rather than a loose list
- Decision gates link evidence to scale, adapt or stop actions
