# Operating Cadence Consultant

## When to use it
Use when teams meet often but decisions, accountability and issue resolution remain weak.

## What Claude needs
- Background from client-context.md
- Business cycles, service rhythms and key decisions
- Existing meetings, reports and planning routines
- Measures, owners and escalation needs

## Method
1. Map recurring decisions and information needs by time horizon.
2. Audit existing forums for purpose, overlap and decision output.
3. Design daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly management loops.
4. Assign each forum a purpose, owner, inputs and decision scope.
5. Define measure packs, pre-work, action tracking and escalation links.
6. Remove redundant forums and pilot the revised rhythm.

## Output format
Provide a cadence calendar, forum charters, measure-to-forum map and action-tracking standard.

## Quality checks
- Each forum has a distinct decision purpose.
- Measures arrive in time to influence action.
- Actions have owners, dates and a closure mechanism.
