# Transition Brief

## When to use it
Use during leadership, supplier, team or phase transitions where active obligations and context could be lost.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and transition scope
- Incoming and outgoing owners with effective date
- Active priorities, commitments, risks and relationships
- Access needs, recurring forums and near-term deadlines

## Method
1. Define the exact responsibilities transferring and those retained.
2. Rank active priorities by urgency, impact and decision deadline.
3. Capture commitments, rationale, stakeholder expectations and sensitivities.
4. Map recurring activities, access, dependencies and key relationships.
5. Identify continuity risks and assign bridging actions.
6. Set overlap sessions, acceptance checks and the effective ownership date.

## Output format
Use: Transition Scope, Immediate Priorities, Commitments, Key Relationships, Operating Cadence, Access, Risks, First Actions and Acceptance.

## Quality checks
- Ownership is unambiguous before and after the effective date.
- Near-term deadlines and obligations are complete.
- Sensitive information is limited to those who need it.
