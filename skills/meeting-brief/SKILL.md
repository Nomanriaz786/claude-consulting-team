# Meeting Brief

## When to use it
Use before a consequential client, board, partner or governance meeting with limited preparation time.

## What Claude needs
- client-context.md and meeting purpose
- Agenda, participants and decision rights
- Relevant history, commitments and sensitivities
- Desired outcome, boundaries and supporting evidence

## Method
1. Define the primary outcome and minimum acceptable outcome.
2. Map participants by position, interest, influence and likely concern.
3. Summarise relevant history, commitments and unresolved points.
4. Develop key messages supported by concise evidence.
5. Anticipate difficult questions and prepare direct responses.
6. Set opening, decision moments, concessions and follow-up actions.

## Output format
Use: Meeting Objective, Participants, Context, Stakeholder Positions, Key Messages, Difficult Questions, Boundaries, Agenda Tactics and Follow-up.

## Quality checks
- Advice reflects known stakeholder positions, not invented motives.
- Key messages align with approved organisational positions.
- Desired decisions and boundaries are specific.
