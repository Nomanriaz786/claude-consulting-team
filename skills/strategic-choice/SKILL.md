# Strategic Choice

## When to use it
Use when evidence exists but competing priorities or incompatible options are preventing commitment.

## What Claude needs
- Strategic objective and decision rights
- Context and capabilities from client-context.md
- Evaluated opportunities and alternatives
- Market, customer and competitor evidence

## Method
1. Frame the choice as mutually exclusive strategic alternatives.
2. Define where-to-play dimensions across customers, needs, offers, channels and geography.
3. Define how-to-win logic for each alternative and its required capabilities.
4. Test alternatives against advantage, coherence, feasibility and risk.
5. Run a pre-mortem on the leading option and identify mitigations.
6. State the chosen direction, rejected alternatives and explicit trade-offs.

## Output format
Provide a choice statement, alternative comparison, where-to-play and how-to-win logic, trade-offs, risks and decision record.

## Quality checks
- The recommendation represents a real choice
- Advantage claims explain why customers choose the business
- Rejected options and consequences are explicit
